# Transistor

**The single most important invention in this whole chain.** A transistor is a [[Semiconductor|silicon]] device with three terminals where a *small signal on one terminal controls a large current between the other two*.

Two ways to think about that:
1. **Amplifier** — a small input controls a bigger output (radios, audio).
2. **Switch** — the input turns the current fully ON or fully OFF. This is the digital use → see [[Transistor as a Switch]].

## Two families
- **BJT** (bipolar junction transistor) — a sandwich of doped regions (NPN/PNP), controlled by a small *current*. Historically first.
- **[[MOSFET]]** — controlled by a *voltage* on an insulated gate. Uses almost no power to hold a state, and shrinks beautifully. **This is what modern chips are built from** — a [[CPU]] is billions of MOSFETs.

## Why it changed everything
Before transistors, a "switch controlled by electricity" meant a bulky, hot, slow vacuum tube or relay. Transistors are tiny, cheap, fast, and can be printed by the billion on one chip. Wire transistors together and you can build [[Logic Gates]] → arithmetic → memory → a computer.
