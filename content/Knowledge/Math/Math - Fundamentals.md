## Functions
- Domain of a function — the set of valid inputs
- **Roots / zeros** — the input values for which the function equals zero
- **End behaviour** — how a function behaves as the input becomes very large or very small
- Interpolation vs extrapolation — estimating within vs beyond the known range

## Statistics
- Mean, Median, Mode
- Scatter plot; trend line / regression line; slope of a line
- **Mean Absolute Deviation** = mean of each value's deviation from the mean
  = mean of (mean − each value)
- **Tail skew** — left tail longer than right ⇒ left-skewed; right tail longer ⇒ right-skewed

## Geometry
- **Triangle inequality theorem** — in a triangle, the length of a side must be
  greater than the difference and less than the sum of the other two sides.
